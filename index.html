Research

Catching market stress as it happens.

Four detectors, updated every five minutes, tested on two decades of SPY. A time-of-day volatility feature improved detection and forecasts. Simple volatility rules matched the models, and I reported that too.

2.2M+one-minute SPY bars, 2003–2026
4detectors: two HMMs, a semi-Markov model and change-point detection
43protocol amendments, each SHA-256 logged
344/344runs reproduced exactly by a separate implementation

What it found

Independent research · Faculty mentor Dr. Cagla Yildirim · Mar 2026 – present

  • Time-of-day-adjusted volatility beat returns-only baselines, replicated on 2019–2022 and 2025–2026.
  • A post hoc benchmark found simple volatility rules matched or beat the detectors.
  • No evidence that SPY switches predict moves in Treasuries, credit, gold or the dollar.

How it was done

Built to be checked

  • 113 candidate features screened to 13, with walk-forward testing.
  • Label-free evaluation: planted volatility breaks, next-hour forecasts, Fed statements.
  • A live forward test is running now. Code, frozen datasets and the full amendment log are public on GitHub. Preprint with Dr. Yildirim: doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24512827.
